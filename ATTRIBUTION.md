# Attribution and third-party data

This project intends to derive data from third-party resources. **No
third-party corpus or lexicon has been imported yet.** Before importing,
record exact upstream version/commit, component-level license, copyright
notice, attribution, modifications, and redistribution conditions.

## Planned sources (not yet incorporated)

- Open Scriptures MorphHB: https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb
- Open Scriptures Hebrew Lexicon: https://github.com/openscriptures/HebrewLexicon

Do not assume all files in an upstream repository share the same license.
Review the relevant source files and license notices before distribution.

## Attribution requirements for downstream consumers

When reusing material licensed under CC BY 4.0, provide the creator/source
attribution, license URL, and indication of changes, as applicable. This
includes commercial apps supported by advertising.
